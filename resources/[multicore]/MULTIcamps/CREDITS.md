# Credits for MULTIcamps

MULTIcamps is GPL v3, part of MULTIcore by RINGO (RINGOscripting). It is MULTIcore's own code.

Ideas and game facts taken from other GPL v3 work:
- The placement preview (a see-through prop in front of the player, turned with the arrow keys,
  confirmed with a hold on the left mouse button and cancelled with the right) follows the idea of
  the prop placer in RSG v2's **rsg-goldclaim** (`client/placeprop.lua`, GPL v3, Rexshack-RedM team
  and contributors), including its control hashes (FrontendLeft / FrontendRight, Attack / Aim).
  The code in `client/main.lua` is written fresh. MULTIcamps is not affiliated with or endorsed by
  the RSG team.
- Town centres in `config/towns.lua` are the ones MULTIdoors uses (`config/towns.lua` there).

Prop models, scenarios and control hashes come from redm-info (`objects.lua`, `Scenarios.lua`,
`Controls.cs`); natives from femga's `natives.json`.
