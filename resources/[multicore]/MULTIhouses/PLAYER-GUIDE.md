# Houses: player guide

Buy a house, keep your things in it, change your outfit there, give keys to friends and keep up with your
payments so you do not lose it.

## Buying a house
1. Find a "For sale" blip on the map and walk to the house.
2. Hold **Left Alt** at the house and pick **View the house**.
3. You pay a **deposit** (20% of the price) in cash. The rest is your **balance**, which you pay off later.
4. **One house per character.** Sell or give up your house before buying another.

## Your house menu
Hold **Left Alt** at your house and pick **House menu**:

- **Guests**: give a key to someone you trust, or take it back.
- **Property Tax**: see your credit, add credit or take some out.
- **Pay Balance**: pay toward what you still owe.
- **Storage**: your house stash (48 slots).
- **Outfits**: change into your saved outfits.
- **Telegrams**: read and send telegrams.
- **Sell House** / **Give Up House**.

While you still owe money on the house you can only pay, sell or give it up. Press **ESC** to close the menu.

## Doors
Hold **Left Alt** at a door of your house and pick **Lock / unlock**. Your guests can do the same.

## Guests
- You need the other player's **citizen ID**; ask them for it.
- Guests can open the storage, change outfits and use the doors.
- Take a key back any time under **Guests > Remove Guest**.

## Paying and tax
- Pay your balance within **30 days** of buying, or the house is taken back. You get a telegram reminder.
- If land tax is on, it is paid from the house's **property credit**. Keep some credit on the house; you get a
  telegram when it runs low.

## Selling
You get **80% of the price**, minus anything you still owe. Keys and storage access go with the house.

## Break-ins
Other players can pick your door's lock with a lockpick and search your storage, so do not keep everything in one place.

*Prices, tax and times are set by the server and may differ from the numbers above.*
