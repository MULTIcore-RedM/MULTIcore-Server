## rsg-consume (RSG v2)
Original project: https://github.com/Rexshack-RedM/rsg-consume
- Copyright (C) Rexshack-RedM and contributors.

Reused (adapted; marked in each file's header):
- `client/use.lua`: the eat, eat-from-can, drink, stew and coffee animations (dicts, clips, flags, props,
  bones and offsets), the mounted item interactions, the stew bowl / spoon and coffee mug interaction calls
  and hashes, waiting for the stew interaction to end.
- `config/anims.lua`, `config.lua`: the same names and offsets as config values; the vomit and sleep
  animations and the screen effects for passing out and waking up.
- `config/consumables.lua`: the props for bread, meat, fish, canned apricots, water and beer.
